## About Us

Community For Better Life is a refugee-led nonprofit organization established in the Dzaleka Refugee Camp. Driven by a profound understanding of the challenges faced by marginalized communities in Malawi. Our mission is to bridge the gap between economic exclusion and environmental degradation, fostering a more inclusive and sustainable future. We envision a world where everyone has equal access to employment opportunities and the empowerment to protect the natural world. By promoting economic inclusion and environmental conservation.

We are inviting suitably qualified and dedicated candidates to fill the vacant position of communications coordinator to support and manage the communication department of the organization.

## Key Responsibilities

- Oversee the organization media channels
- Content creation and management
- Manage internal and external communications
- Plan and organize the team virtual meetings

## Requirements

- Certificate in content creation or related field
- Information and Technology skills
- Proficiency in Microsoft products
- Minimum 2 years of experience in content creation
- Strong written and verbal communication and interpersonal skills
- Ability to adapt and manage our website and other communication platforms
