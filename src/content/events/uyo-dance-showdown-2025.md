## Event Details

The UYO Dance Showdown 2025 National Qualifiers is set to be an electrifying dance competition featuring the renowned Black Snake Dance Crew and special performances by Cretik and surprise guest artists.

### Featured Contestant: Black Snake Dance Crew

Founded in 2014 in Dzaleka Refugee Camp (Dowa District), Black Snake Dance Crew is a dynamic team of 24 passionate dancers. Their name symbolizes fearless movement, resilience, and the power of unity through dance.

#### Biography

Born out of a desire to uplift and empower youth in the camp, the crew brings together diverse backgrounds to perform:
- Afro dance
- Hip-hop
- Cultural styles
- Freestyle

Their performances are known for powerful storytelling and emotional depth, highlighting themes of peace, identity, and hope.

#### Notable Achievements
- Tumaini Festival performances
- KIU Awards appearances
- Carithelove Festival
- Yetu Radio Talent Show

#### Vision
As UMP Dance Ambassador 2025 candidates, they aim to:
- Represent refugee voices
- Promote youth empowerment
- Showcase dance as a tool for unity and change
