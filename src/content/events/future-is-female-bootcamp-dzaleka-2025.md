## About the Bootcamp

The Future is Female Bootcamp is a 5-day co-creative experience in music, fashion, and dance, designed for female creatives living in Dzaleka Refugee Camp. This unique program is part of the European Spaces of Culture initiative and is led by Tumaini Letu, with curriculum design by Zomba Arts Platform. The bootcamp brings together 10 talented women (ages 18–35) for hands-on training, cross-discipline collaboration, and a public showcase.

### When & Where
- **Dates:** 28 July – 1 August 2025
- **Time:** 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM daily
- **Location:** Dzaleka Refugee Camp
- **Cost:** Free (limited to 10 participants)

### Why Join?
- Hands-on training in DJing, fashion design, or dance
- Daily cross-discipline co-creation with fellow women artists
- Build and perform a live showcase piece from scratch
- Receive a certificate of participation
- Access mentorship on turning your art into income

### What Makes This Bootcamp Unique?
- **Expert Design, Local Delivery:** Zomba Arts Platform crafted the curriculum, Tumaini Letu adapts it for Dzaleka’s context.
- **True Co-Creation:** Interdisciplinary teams research a social theme, develop concepts, and build an integrated final performance.
- **Hands-On Mentorship:** Morning masterclasses, afternoon studio labs, and ongoing peer feedback.
- **Community Showcase:** Culminates in a public performance in Dzaleka, featuring live music, custom costumes, and choreography by participants.

### Who Should Apply?
- Women aged 18–35 living in Dzaleka Camp
- Passionate about music, fashion, or dance
- Eager to learn, collaborate, and perform
