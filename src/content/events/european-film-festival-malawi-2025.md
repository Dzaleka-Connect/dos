## About the Festival

The European Film Festival in Malawi brings together a diverse collection of European films that tell compelling stories about love, betrayal, identity, and survival. Each film offers a unique perspective into our shared human experiences.

## Event Details

The festival will take place across three venues in Lilongwe and Dzaleka Refugee Camp:

1. **EU Residence** (June 5, 2025)
   - Opening night of the festival
   - Special screening and reception

2. **Crossroads Hotel Auditorium** (June 7, 2025)
   - Main festival screenings
   - Cinema experience in the heart of Lilongwe

3. **Salama Africa Hall, Dzaleka Refugee Camp** (June 8, 2025)
   - Special screening for the refugee community
   - Cultural exchange through film

## Film Selection

The festival features a carefully curated selection of European films that showcase:
- Contemporary European cinema
- Diverse storytelling perspectives
- Cultural exchange between Europe and Malawi
- Stories of human connection and resilience

## Organizer

This event is organized by the European Union Delegation to Malawi, bringing European culture and arts to Malawian audiences while fostering cultural exchange and understanding.
