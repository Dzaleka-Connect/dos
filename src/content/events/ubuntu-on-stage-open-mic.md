## About the event

Ubuntu on Stage — *Dzaleka speaks* — is an open mic for poets, singers, comedians, and storytellers in Dzaleka. It welcomes performers from the camp's different national communities, and first-time performers are encouraged alongside experienced ones.

The event uses art to support healing, dignity, and identity for people living in displacement, and brings performers and audiences from different backgrounds onto one stage.

## Details

- **Date:** 6 September 2026
- **Time:** 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM (Malawi time)
- **Venue:** Near the Plan library, Dzaleka Refugee Camp
- **Capacity:** Around 200 people
- **Registration:** Not required — performers and audience are welcome to attend
