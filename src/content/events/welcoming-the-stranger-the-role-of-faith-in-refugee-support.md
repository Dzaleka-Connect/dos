## Event Overview

Sacred texts across traditions call on believers to welcome strangers — including refugees and displaced communities. In this session, Malawi’s faith leaders reflect on how communities can stand with refugees through compassion, advocacy, and action.

## About This Session

This September 2025 conversation explores how faith communities in Malawi have responded to the call to welcome the stranger, standing in solidarity with refugees through practical support and public witness. What contributions have they made? What challenges remain? And how can faith-based support grow stronger and more impactful?

## Why This Matters

- Center faith-based compassion and service for displaced people  
- Share best practices across traditions  
- Identify opportunities for deeper collaboration and advocacy
