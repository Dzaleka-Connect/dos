## Zoom Meeting Overview

Join Inua for an important Zoom session on Education at Dzaleka Refugee Camp! Discover what education truly means for people living in Dzaleka Refugee Camp—the opportunities it brings and the barriers they face. Hear directly from those who have overcome these challenges, sharing their stories of resilience and hope.

This session will explore:
- The current state of education in Dzaleka Refugee Camp
- Success stories of students who have overcome challenges
- Available educational opportunities and resources
- Ways to support education initiatives in the camp
