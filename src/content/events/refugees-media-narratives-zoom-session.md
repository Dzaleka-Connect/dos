## About This Session

Refugees in Malawi face daily struggles, many rooted in outdated and limiting policies. The media has the power to change public perception, influence policy, and amplify voices that often go unheard.

This conversation will reflect on how Malawi's media has shaped the national narrative around refugees—celebrating impactful reporting while exploring what more can be done. We'll discuss how journalism can continue to spotlight refugee experiences, drive accountability, and help shape a future where refugee rights and human dignity are respected.

## Discussion Topics

- **Media Representation:** How refugees are portrayed in Malawian media
- **Policy Influence:** The role of journalism in shaping refugee policies
- **Voice Amplification:** Ensuring refugee perspectives are heard
- **Best Practices:** Celebrating impactful refugee reporting
- **Future Directions:** What more can be done to protect rights and dignity

## Why This Matters

Let's talk about how journalism can inspire change and shape a better future for refugees in Malawi. This session aims to:

- Foster dialogue between media professionals and refugee advocates
- Identify opportunities for improved refugee representation
- Explore collaborative approaches to policy change
- Celebrate successful media initiatives that have made a difference
