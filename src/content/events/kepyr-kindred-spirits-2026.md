## Event Details

### In-Person | Friday | June 19 | 9-11am ET

On June 19, **KEPYR** brings the kids’ media community into real-time creative connection with the extraordinary young poets in **Malawi’s Dzaleka Refugee Camp**. Through Shared Studios’ innovative Live Portal technology, kids from the youth-led collective AYAP (African Youth Artistic Poetry) will perform their work live, appearing as if they were standing right beside us.

In this immersive morning of performances, borders dissolve and voices travel farther than circumstance.
