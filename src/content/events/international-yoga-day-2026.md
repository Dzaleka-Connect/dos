## Event Overview

Join Art for Hope and the Dzaleka community in celebrating the International Day of Yoga 2026. This year, we honor the journey of thousands within our Dzaleka community who have turned to yoga as a tool for healing, recovery from trauma, and inner peace.

What started in 2016 as a personal path to recovery has grown into a movement reaching over 5,000 students and residents monthly. This event is a powerful showcase of our collective strength, featuring:

- Mass Yoga Practice: A unified session open to all ages and backgrounds.
- Youth Performances: Special demonstrations by students from our 30 partner schools and the Amahoro drummers.
- Healing Stories: Reflections on how yoga helps us rebuild our lives and find "sleep after the storm."

Beyond the physical postures, this is a day to prove that wellness has no borders and that even in marginalized spaces, hope and health can flourish.
