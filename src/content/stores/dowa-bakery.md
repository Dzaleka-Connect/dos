## Our Story

The gap in the market for bread was huge until Ahmed Hassan, a Somali refugee, set up Dowa Bakery.

Today the bakery employs **28 people**: 14 Malawian and 14 refugees originally from Somalia, Rwanda, Burundi, and the Democratic Republic of Congo. Up to **375 kg of flour per day** are required to meet the production needs.

## What We Offer

Dowa Bakery has expanded their product range far beyond the standard loaf:

- **8 types of bread** - From standard loaves to specialty varieties
- **Biscuits** - Freshly baked daily
- **Various doughnuts** - Different types to choose from
- **Communion wafers** - For churches and religious services

## Impact on the Community

Set up with personal funds and a great deal of entrepreneurial spirit, Dowa Bakery is a great example of the positive impact which refugee businesses can have on local economy and employment.

**Ingredients**: Wheat Flour, Salt, Sugar, Soya Flour, Vegetable Fat, Emulsifiers, Bread Improver, Preservatives, Calcium Propionate, Vitamins, Water, Yeast.
