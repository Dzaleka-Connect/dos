## About Holistic Focus for the Marginalized

Holistic Focus for the Marginalized provides education, health, and livelihood support for vulnerable groups.

The organization takes a holistic approach to addressing the needs of marginalized communities, focusing on long-term sustainable solutions.

For more information or to get involved, please contact them through their Facebook page or via phone.
