## About Corneille Orphanage

We are Corneille Orphanage, located at DZALEKA Refugee Camp in Malawi. We are an organization dedicated to caring for refugee children who have lost their parents and have nowhere to go.

Life at DZALEKA Refugee Camp is difficult and precarious. The children we welcome have experienced traumatic events, having lost their families and homes. They have had to flee their country to escape violence and persecution. Despite the efforts of humanitarian organizations to help them, these children continue to face many challenges.

## Our Mission

Our orphanage was created to provide a safe and stable refuge for these children. We provide them with:
- Shelter and housing
- Nutritious meals
- Clothing and basic necessities
- Access to education
- A family environment where they can feel safe
- Support to rebuild their lives
