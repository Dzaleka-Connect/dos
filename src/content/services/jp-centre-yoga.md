## About JP Centre Yoga

JP Centre Yoga is a local, independent and community-minded yoga studio located in the heart of Jamaica Plain since 2012. The studio operates in a spacious, converted warehouse with high ceilings and large windows, creating an inspiring environment for practice. Known for its supportive, holistic approach to yoga and wellness, JP Centre Yoga emphasizes inclusivity, compassion, and community connection.

## Key Services:

- **In-Studio Yoga Classes**: Join classes in person at their Jamaica Plain location 7 days a week
- **Virtual Classes**: Practice via livestream with real-time Zoom sessions or access recorded classes available for 48 hours
- **Teacher Training Programs**: Comprehensive 200-hour foundations training, advanced 300-hour training, and specialized programs including trauma-informed yoga therapy
- **Holistic Healing**: Shiatsu healing, therapeutic massage, and acupuncture services
- **Meditation & Mindfulness**: Regular meditation classes and mindfulness courses
- **Community Events**: Special workshops, seasonal practices, and BIPOC-only spaces

## Special Programs:

- **Yoga Equity Project**: Promoting accessibility and inclusion in yoga practice
- **Dzaleka Refugee Teacher Training**: Specialized training program for refugee communities
- **Workstudy Program**: Opportunities for community members to contribute and practice
- **Fellowship & Scholarship Programs**: Making yoga accessible to all community members

## Notable Features:

- **Community Focus**: Welcoming, inclusive, and affirming environment for all practitioners
- **Flexible Practice Options**: Choose between in-person, livestream, or recorded sessions
- **Experienced Instructors**: Led by Daniel Max and other dedicated teachers with calm, supportive, and methodical approaches
- **Comprehensive Wellness**: Beyond yoga, offering nutrition services and various healing modalities

## How to Practice:

- **In-Studio**: Join classes in person at their Jamaica Plain location
- **Virtual**: Sign up for livestream classes and receive Zoom links, or access recorded classes if you miss the live session
- **Special Events**: Participate in seasonal workshops, meditation courses, and community gatherings

For class schedules, teacher training information, and to book sessions, visit [jpcentreyoga.com](https://www.jpcentreyoga.com/).
