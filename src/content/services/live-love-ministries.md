## About Live Love Ministries

Live Love Ministries is a 501c3 non-profit organization. All of our US based missionaries are support raising missionaries endorsed by the North American Mission Board. Our vision is that every community in which we serve would have consistent access to the good news of Jesus. All of our ministry efforts are to see that vision become a reality.

## Key Services:

- Community Outreach: Spreading the good news of Jesus in local communities
- Missionary Support: Supporting US-based missionaries in their ministry work
- Religious Education: Providing religious education and guidance
- Community Development: Working towards community betterment through faith-based initiatives

## Recent Milestones:
- Endorsed by the North American Mission Board
- Established presence in Dzaleka Refugee Camp
- Supporting local community through faith-based initiatives

For more information or to support their initiatives, visit [liveloveministries.org](https://liveloveministries.org/).
