## About Humanitas CLUB 

We are committed to providing vital aid and holistic support to pregnant women by focusing on maternal health, nutrition, and overall well-being during and after pregnancy. We also stand with widows and orphans, offering them care, resources, and empowerment opportunities to improve their quality of life and restore hope for the future.
