## About Women Shine Organization

Women Shine Organization is a refugee-led non-profit dedicated to empowering women and girls, supporting orphans, and promoting inclusivity for people with disabilities in Dzaleka Refugee Camp. Through education, skills training, mentorship, and community-driven programs, they aim to break barriers, foster independence, and create brighter futures.

### Mission
To empower women and girls, support orphans, and include people with disabilities through education, skills training, mentorship, and community-driven programs to break barriers, foster independence, and create brighter futures.

### Vision
A society where every woman, girl, orphan, and person with a disability is empowered, included, and equipped to live with dignity, purpose, and hope.

### Core Programs

*   **Skills Training & Capacity Building:** Vocational training in tailoring, beadwork, soap making, baking, hairdressing, digital literacy, farming, and financial literacy.
*   **Psychosocial Support & Mental Health:** Counseling, peer support groups, art therapy, and cultural healing activities.
*   **Economic Empowerment:** Entrepreneurship support through start-up kits, mentorship, savings groups (VSLAs), and market linkages.
*   **Health & Wellness:** Menstrual hygiene management, nutrition education, and disability-inclusive health workshops.
*   **Girl & Orphan Support:** Provision of school fees, uniforms, tutoring, safe play spaces, and psychosocial care.
*   **Disability Inclusion:** Inclusive training programs, accessibility advocacy, and peer confidence building.
*   **Advocacy & Awareness:** Campaigns focused on GBV prevention, child protection, disability rights, and youth leadership.
