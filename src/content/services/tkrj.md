## About Together Knowledge Resources Joins (TKRJ)

Together Knowledge Resources Joins (TKRJ) is a Christian faith-based NGO founded by Theophile Kizungu in 2019 at Dzaleka Refugee Camp. Our purpose is to develop, empower, and rise talents across vulnerable communities, turning hidden potential into sustainable livelihoods and spiritual growth. Guided by the principle "Let all that you do be done in love" (1 Corinthians 16:14), TKRJ is more than a development NGO — we are a Christian family committed to spreading the Gospel while providing practical support to those in need.

## Key Services:

- **Living Word Ministry**: Sharing the Gospel through Bible studies, prayer, and discipleship programs
- **Vocational Training**: Comprehensive skills development in carpentry, farming, tailoring, and ICT
- **Carpentry Workshop**: Empowering refugees and youth with practical skills and sustainable income opportunities
- **Humanitarian Support**: Feeding programs, crisis relief, and community empowerment initiatives
- **Community Development**: Uplifting women, orphans, refugees, and persons with disabilities
- **Christian Education**: Faith-based learning and spiritual development programs

## Our Christian Programs:

- **Spreading the Gospel & Christian Education**: Bible studies, prayer groups, and discipleship training
- **Vocational Training Programs**: 
  - Carpentry skills development
  - Sustainable farming techniques
  - Tailoring and fashion design
  - Information and Communication Technology (ICT)
- **Feeding the Hungry & Caring for the Poor**: Food distribution and poverty alleviation programs
- **Community Empowerment**: Supporting vulnerable groups including women, orphans, refugees, and persons with disabilities

## Mission & Vision:

- **Mission**: Empowering communities to rise, develop skills, and live with dignity through Christ-centered love and service
- **Vision**: We inspire smarter empowerment for greater impact
- **Core Values**: Faith, love, service, empowerment, and community development

## Geographic Presence:

- **Malawi**: Dzaleka Refugee Camp (Primary location)
- **Uganda**: Nakivale Settlement
- **Australia**: Administrative and support operations
