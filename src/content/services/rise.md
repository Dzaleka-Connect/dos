## About Refugee Independent Schools Association (RISA)

Refugee Independent Schools Association (RISA) is a non-governmental organization whose aim and objective is to unify all private schools in Dzaleka refugee camp. Our goal is to promote high-quality education by respecting the Malawi national syllabus as well as the law to promote cultural integration and sustainable development within the Dzaleka community through education.

## Our Mission

To unify all private schools in Dzaleka refugee camp to promote high quality education. We strive to train teachers, support member schools, and ensure every student has access to quality education in a safe and disciplined environment.

## What We Do

- **Promoting Quality Education**: Ensuring schools adhere to national standards and providing the best learning environment.
- **Supporting Member Schools**: Offering training, resources, and unifying private educational institutions.
- **Community Engagement**: Working with families and the community to support student success.
