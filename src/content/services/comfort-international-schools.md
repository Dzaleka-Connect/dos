## About Comfort International Schools

Comfort the Community (CC) is a community-based organization in Dzaleka refugee camp, dedicated to promoting socio-economic empowerment through strategic interventions and sustainable development programs. We provide education, empowerment, and care for disadvantaged and poverty-stricken communities, with a special focus on children with disabilities, orphans, and girls vulnerable to early marriages.

Comfort International Schools offers nursery (pre-school), primary 1-8, and secondary 1-4 education to refugee and host community children.
