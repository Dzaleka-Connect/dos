## About The Roots of Hope - ROHO 

ROHO," a Swahili word for "heart," is also an acronym for "The Roots of Hope." Our mission is to provide hope and support to marginalized communities, focusing on vulnerable populations such as refugees, orphans, and those impacted by poverty and conflicts.
