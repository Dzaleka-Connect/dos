## About Sauti ya Mama Ltd

Sauti ya Mama Ltd (registration number COYG-MK7TRNR) is a community organization operating in Dzaleka Refugee Camp and neighboring host villages in Dowa District. Its motto is *"Sauti ya Haki, Ustawi, na Uhuru"* ("The Voice of Justice, Prosperity, and Freedom").

Its mission is empowering displaced and host community women and youth through economic sovereignty, gender-based violence prevention, and child protection.

## Core initiatives

- **Sabuni ya Ustawi** — eco-soap manufacturing and micro-enterprise development for vulnerable women.
- **Akadema ya Mama** — community homeschooling hubs providing foundational education for out-of-school children.
- **Sauti ya Baba** — male engagement circles promoting active parenting and healthy household dynamics.
