## About Incubation Network Africa

Incubation Network Africa (INA) is a community-driven initiative located in Dzaleka Refugee Camp. It focuses on empowering youth and families by providing them with essential skills in technology, creativity, and entrepreneurship. Their mission is to turn curiosity into skills and skills into opportunities for lasting success.

### Programs

INA offers several programs designed to foster innovation and career readiness:

*   **Computer Literacy Program**: Students gain essential digital skills, mastering basic computer operations, productivity software, and navigating the digital world confidently.
*   **Graphic Design Program**: Participants learn design principles, software tools, and creative techniques to produce impactful and professional visual content.
*   **6-Month Intensive Training**: A comprehensive program combining computer literacy and graphic design to prepare youth for tech-driven careers.

For more information, visit their [website](https://incubationnetworkafrica.org/).
