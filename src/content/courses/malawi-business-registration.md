## Module 1: Introduction to MBRS

### 1.1 What is MBRS?
The **Malawi Business Registration System (MBRS)** is a digital platform operated by the Department of Registrar General (under the Ministry of Justice). It replaces the old manual paper-based system, allowing you to register your business from anywhere.

**Official Website:** [mbrs.gov.mw](https://mbrs.gov.mw)

### 1.2 Types of Registration
Before you begin, decide what you are registering:
1.  **Business Name**: For Sole Proprietorships (one owner) or Partnerships. Simpler and cheaper.
2.  **Limited Company**: A separate legal entity from its owners. Requires Directors and Shareholders.

### 1.3 Required Documents Checklist
Gather these before you start:
-   **National ID** (for citizens) or **Passport/Resident Permit** (for non-citizens).
-   **Digital Passport Photo** (for your profile).
-   **Proposed Business Names** (have 3 options in case your first choice is taken).
-   **Contact Details**: Email address and phone number.
-   **Physical Address**: Plot number or clear physical location description.

## Module 2: Creating Your MBRS Account

### 2.1 Registration Steps
1.  Go to [mbrs.gov.mw](https://mbrs.gov.mw).
2.  Select **"CREATE ACCOUNT"**.
3.  Select **"Citizen"** or **"Non-Citizen"**.
    -   *Note: Citizens must use their National ID number.*
4.  Fill in your personal details exactly as they appear on your ID.
5.  Create a strong password and submit.

### 2.2 Verification
-   You may need to verify your email address.
-   Once verified, log in to access your dashboard.

## Module 3: Registering a Business Name

*Best for: Small businesses, freelancers, and sole traders.*

### 3.1 The Application Process
1.  Log in and select **"Business Name Registration"**.
2.  **Name Search**: Enter your proposed business name to check availability.
3.  **Business Details**: Enter the nature of business (what you do) and principal place of business.
4.  **Ownership**: Enter details of the owner(s).

### 3.2 Fees
-   **Application Fee**: Approximately **MWK 2,000** (subject to change).
-   Payment is made via bank or mobile money (see Module 5).

## Module 4: Incorporating a Limited Company

*Best for: Growing businesses, startups seeking investment, and formal organisations.*

### 4.1 The Incorporation Process
1.  Log in and select **"Company Incorporation"**.
2.  **Company Details**:
    -   Company Type (Private Limited, Public).
    -   Share Capital (for example, 100,000 shares).
3.  **Directors & Shareholders**:
    -   Add at least 2 Directors (for private companies).
    -   Add Shareholders and allocate shares.
4.  **Company Secretary**: Required for companies.

### 4.2 Generated Documents
The system will auto-generate these PDF documents for you:
-   **Memorandum of Association**
-   **Articles of Association**
-   **Consent of Directors & Shareholders**

**Action Required**: You must **PRINT** these documents, **SIGN** them, and **SCAN** them back into the system.

### 4.3 Beneficial Ownership
You must declare who the ultimate owners of the company are (the "Beneficial Owners"). This is a mandatory legal requirement.

## Module 5: Payments and Certification

### 5.1 Making Payments
After submitting your application, you will get an **Application Number**.
-   **Bank Payment**: Visit **FDH Bank** (or other designated banks) with your application number.
-   **Online Payment**: Use the integrated payment gateway if available (Visa/Mastercard/Mobile Money).

**Estimated Fees (Company)**: ~MWK 75,000 (check official site for latest fees).

### 5.2 Getting Your Certificate
-   Once payment is confirmed, the Registrar General reviews your application.
-   **Approval Time**: Typically 3-5 working days.
-   **Download**: Log in to your dashboard to download your **Certificate of Incorporation** or **Business Registration Certificate**.

## Module 6: Post-Registration Obligations

Registration is just the beginning!

### 6.1 Tax Registration (MRA)
You must register with the **Malawi Revenue Authority (MRA)**.
-   Get your **TPIN** (Taxpayer Identification Number).
-   Register for VAT (if turnover > MWK 25m/year) and PAYE (if you have employees).
-   [MRA Website](https://www.mra.mw)

### 6.2 Business Bank Account
Take your Certificate of Incorporation and TPIN to a bank to open a corporate account. Do not use your personal account for business!

### 6.3 Annual Returns
-   **Business Names**: Renew every 3 years.
-   **Companies**: File Annual Returns every year with the Registrar General to keep your company active.
