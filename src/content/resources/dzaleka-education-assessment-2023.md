## Overview

This assessment examines the state of education in Dzaleka Refugee Camp, focusing on access to quality education, learning outcomes, and challenges faced by refugee children and youth in pursuing their educational goals.

## Key Areas Covered

- School enrollment rates
- Educational infrastructure
- Teaching quality and resources
- Learning outcomes
- Barriers to education
- Special education needs
- Vocational training
- Higher education access
- Gender parity in education
- Teacher qualifications

## Methodology

- Mixed-methods approach
- School-based surveys
- Classroom observations
- Stakeholder interviews
- Student assessments
- Parent and community consultations

## Key Findings

The assessment revealed:
- Enrollment and attendance patterns
- Quality of education delivery
- Resource constraints and needs
- Success factors and challenges
- Community engagement in education
- Impact of COVID-19 on learning

## Significance

This study informs:
- Education policy development
- Resource allocation decisions
- Teacher training needs
- Infrastructure planning
- Program design and implementation
- Partnership opportunities
