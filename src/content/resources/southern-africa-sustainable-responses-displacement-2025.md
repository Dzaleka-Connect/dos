## Background

This 2025 UNHCR report provides an in-depth analysis of sustainable responses to displacement across Southern Africa. It examines regional trends, policy frameworks, and best practices for protection, integration, and durable solutions for refugees and internally displaced persons. The report highlights collaborative efforts among governments, humanitarian organizations, and affected communities to address the root causes of displacement and promote long-term stability in the region.
