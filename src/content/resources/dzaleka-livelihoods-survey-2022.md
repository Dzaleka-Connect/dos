## Overview

This survey examines the livelihood strategies, economic activities, and self-reliance initiatives of refugees in Dzaleka camp, with a focus on identifying opportunities for sustainable economic inclusion and development.

## Key Areas Covered

- Employment and income generation
- Business ownership and entrepreneurship
- Skills and vocational training
- Market access and integration
- Financial inclusion
- Agricultural activities
- Professional qualifications
- Economic barriers and enablers
- Host community economic relations
- Digital literacy and access

## Methodology

- Household surveys
- Business mapping
- Market assessments
- Skills inventories
- Focus group discussions
- Key informant interviews

## Key Findings

The survey revealed:
- Main income sources and activities
- Business development needs
- Skills gaps and opportunities
- Market linkages and barriers
- Financial service access
- Technology adoption levels
- Training and support needs

## Significance

This research supports:
- Economic inclusion strategies
- Skills development programs
- Market-based interventions
- Financial inclusion initiatives
- Private sector partnerships
- Policy advocacy efforts
