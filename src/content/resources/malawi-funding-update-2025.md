## Overview

This report provides a detailed analysis of UNHCR's funding situation for refugee operations in Malawi as of May 31, 2025. The total financial requirements for 2025 amount to $26.3 million, with current funding at 14% ($3,807,432), leaving a significant funding gap of $22,501,046 (86%).

## Key Areas Covered

- Financial requirements for Malawi operations
- Direct donor contributions to Malawi
- Funding gaps and priorities
- Program implementation status
- Emergency response funding
- Protection and assistance needs
- Resource allocation strategies

## Current Status

### Financial Overview
- Total Requirements: $26.3 million
- Current Funding: $3,807,432 (14%)
- Funding Gap: $22,501,046 (86%)

### Direct Contributions to Malawi
1. Japan: $300,000 (Tightly earmarked)
2. United States of America: $73,238 (Earmarked)
3. United Nations Children's Fund: $37,627 (Tightly earmarked)
4. Canada: $35,234 (Tightly earmarked)
5. Sub-total Direct Contributions: $446,099

### Indicative Allocations for Malawi
- Unearmarked: $1,047,416
- Softly earmarked: $469,881
- Adjustments: ($4,470)
- Tightly earmarked: $1,848,506
- Total Indicative Allocation: $3,361,333
