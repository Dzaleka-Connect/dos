## Overview

This report uses Dzaleka Refugee Camp's inclusion in the 2024 Malawi Demographic and Health Survey to compare camp-level indicators with results from the rest of Malawi. It was prepared by Malawi's National Statistical Office and UNHCR, with technical and financial support from the World Bank-UNHCR Joint Data Center on Forced Displacement.

## Coverage

The report covers vaccination, antenatal care, sanitation, birth registration, internet use, literacy, basic water access, employment, family planning, overcrowding, child nutrition, and other health and population indicators.
