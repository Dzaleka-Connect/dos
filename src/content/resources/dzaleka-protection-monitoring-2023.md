## Overview

Regular protection monitoring assessment conducted to understand the protection environment, identify risks, and monitor the well-being of persons of concern in Dzaleka Refugee Camp.

## Key Areas Covered

- Safety and security conditions
- Access to basic services
- Legal documentation
- Child protection
- Gender-based violence
- Access to justice
- Community relations
- Psychosocial support

## Methodology

- Systematic monitoring approach
- Mixed methods: surveys, interviews, focus groups
- Regular data collection cycles
- Community-based protection monitoring
- Participatory assessment techniques

## Key Findings

The monitoring revealed insights about:
- Protection challenges and risks
- Access to services and assistance
- Community coping mechanisms
- Emerging protection concerns
- Service gaps and needs

## Significance

This monitoring helps:
- Identify protection gaps
- Inform programming decisions
- Guide resource allocation
- Support advocacy efforts
- Enable early warning
- Track protection trends

Protection monitoring data available through UNHCR's Microdata Library, with appropriate confidentiality measures to protect sensitive information.
