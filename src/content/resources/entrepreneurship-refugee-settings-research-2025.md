This peer-reviewed academic article published in a Taylor & Francis journal examines entrepreneurship in refugee displacement settings. The research provides valuable insights into:

- Business formation and survival in camp environments
- Economic resilience strategies among displaced populations
- Policy implications for supporting refugee entrepreneurship
- Lessons applicable to Dzaleka Refugee Camp and similar contexts

This resource is valuable for researchers, policymakers, and practitioners working on refugee economic empowerment programs.
