This July 2026 country brief provides comprehensive operational and financial updates on the World Food Programme's (WFP) activities in Malawi, with critical figures concerning refugee food aid in Dzaleka Refugee Camp, emergency flood response, nutrition, and school feeding programs.

## Key Operational & Financial Findings

### 1. Dzaleka Refugee Assistance & Ration Cut Risks
- **Ration Threat**: Over **63,000 refugees and asylum seekers** in Dzaleka Refugee Camp face severe food insecurity from September 2026 due to acute funding shortfalls.
- **Painful Prioritization**: WFP and UNHCR are considering prioritization measures that may exclude a majority of refugees who depend on food assistance due to a lack of alternative livelihood options.
- **Funding Needed**:
  - **$6.8 Million USD**: Required to sustain *reduced* rations through January 2027.
  - **$11.4 Million USD**: Required to restore *full* rations to camp residents.

### 2. Overall Financial Outlook (July - December 2026)
- **Total 2026 Portfolio Requirement**: USD 86.75 Million (only **22% funded** to date with USD 18.65 Million received).
- **Net Funding Shortfall**: **USD 68 Million** ($67.56M exact) required across all operations between July and December 2026.

### 3. School Feeding & Nutrition Operations
- **School Meals Risk**: WFP currently reaches 769,150 learners. Without an injection of **$19.7 Million USD**, nearly **594,000 learners** will lose access to daily school meals in September 2026.
- **Nutrition Deficit**: Moderate acute malnutrition treatment currently serving 134,000+ vulnerable people will run out of resources by November 2026 without immediate funding.

### 4. Climate Shocks & Returnee Influx
- **El Niño Forecast**: An 80%+ probability of a strong El Niño event in the 2026/2027 season threatens crop production, particularly in southern districts.
- **South Africa Returnees**: Over **38,000 Malawians** have returned from South Africa following anti-foreigner attacks, placing extreme pressure on reception centers and basic services.
- **March 2026 Flood Recovery**: WFP continues supporting 25,499 flood-affected households in Chikwawa District with specialized nutrition and oil distributions.
