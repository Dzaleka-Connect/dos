## Background

The Constitution of the Republic of Malawi, first adopted in 1994 and amended through 2021, serves as the supreme law of the country. It establishes the framework for governance, the separation of powers, and the protection of fundamental rights and freedoms for all individuals within Malawi's jurisdiction. The Constitution prohibits discrimination in any form and guarantees equal and effective protection under the law, regardless of race, gender, religion, or other status. It is a cornerstone for the rule of law, democracy, and the protection of human rights in Malawi.
