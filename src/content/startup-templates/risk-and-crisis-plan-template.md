title: Risk and crisis plan template
shortTitle: Risk and crisis plan
description: Prepare for disruptions by listing business risks, likely impacts, prevention steps, emergency contacts, and backup options before a crisis happens.
bestFor: Businesses affected by supply problems, illness, market disruption, weather events, theft, or equipment failure
estimatedTime: 25 to 35 minutes
intro: Use this template to think ahead about what could interrupt your business and what you will do if it happens. A simple risk plan can protect income, customers, and stock.
order: 4
keywords: risk plan template Dzaleka, business continuity Malawi, crisis planning refugee entrepreneurs, small business risk template Dzaleka
whoShouldUse:
  - Small businesses that depend on stock deliveries, seasonal demand, or one key person.
  - Traders and service providers who need backup communication or supplier options.
  - Businesses that want to be more ready for emergencies or unstable market periods.
beforeYouStart:
  - Think about the last 12 months and list the disruptions that already affected your business.
  - Identify the people, suppliers, or equipment your business depends on most.
  - Collect current contact numbers for suppliers, customers, helpers, and emergency support.
sections:
  - title: Main business risks
    description: Start by naming the risks that are most realistic for your business.
    rows:
      - label: Business risk
        prompt: What risk could affect your business?
      - label: What could happen
        prompt: What would the problem look like in real life?
      - label: Likely impact
        prompt: How serious would the impact be on income, stock, or customers?
  - title: Prevention steps
    description: List the actions that reduce the chance of the risk happening or reduce the damage.
    rows:
      - label: Prevention action
        prompt: What can you do now to reduce this risk?
      - label: Who will check it
        prompt: Who is responsible for this prevention step?
      - label: Review frequency
        prompt: How often will you review this risk?
  - title: Response plan
    description: Decide what you will do if the problem happens anyway.
    rows:
      - label: First response
        prompt: What is the first thing you will do?
      - label: Customer communication
        prompt: How will you inform customers or partners?
      - label: Recovery step
        prompt: What helps the business return to normal faster?
  - title: Emergency contacts and backups
    description: Keep essential names and alternatives in one place.
    rows:
      - label: Emergency contacts
        prompt: Which key phone numbers should be ready?
      - label: Backup suppliers
        prompt: Who else can supply you if the main supplier fails?
      - label: Backup communication methods
        prompt: If one channel fails, what will you use instead?
  - title: Crisis review
    description: Decide how you will learn from a disruption after it happens.
    rows:
      - label: What happened
        prompt: What actually happened during the crisis?
      - label: What worked
        prompt: Which response steps helped most?
      - label: What must change
        prompt: What should be improved before the next disruption?
example:
  businessName: Limbikani Beauty Corner
  businessType: Hair and beauty services
  summary: A beauty service provider wants to plan for supply shortages, illness, and phone network disruption.
  sections:
    - title: Main risks
      items:
        - "Risk: hair product supplier delays restocking for two weeks."
        - "Impact: popular services cannot be delivered on time and customers go elsewhere."
        - "Risk: owner becomes ill and cannot work for several days."
    - title: Prevention and response
      items:
        - "Keep one backup supplier for braiding hair and treatment products."
        - "Maintain a short list of trusted assistants who can cover simple appointments."
        - "Message booked customers immediately if delays happen and offer a new time."
    - title: Backups and contacts
      items:
        - "Emergency contacts include the main supplier, backup supplier, assistant, and top repeat customers."
        - "Backup communication methods include SMS if WhatsApp data is unavailable."
        - "Review this plan at the start of every month."
