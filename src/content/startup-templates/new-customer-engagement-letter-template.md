---
title: New customer engagement letter template
shortTitle: Customer engagement letter
description: Use a simple written agreement to confirm the work, price, timeline, approvals, and communication method before you begin.
bestFor: Tailoring, design, catering, photography, tutoring, beauty services, repairs, and freelance digital work
estimatedTime: 15 to 25 minutes
intro: Use this template when a customer has agreed to work with you and you want to confirm the main terms clearly. A simple engagement letter helps avoid confusion about what is included, what it costs, and when payment is due.
order: 5
keywords: customer engagement letter template Dzaleka, service agreement small business Malawi, freelance agreement refugee entrepreneurs, Dzaleka business templates
whoShouldUse:
  - Service providers who deliver work over a few days or weeks.
  - Businesses that need customer approval before work begins.
  - Entrepreneurs who want a simple written record of what was agreed.
beforeYouStart:
  - Confirm the customer’s name, phone number, and preferred contact method.
  - Be clear about the work you are providing and what is not included.
  - Decide the deposit, final payment terms, and how changes will be handled.
sections:
  - title: Customer and business details
    description: Record the basic contact information clearly at the top of the letter.
    rows:
      - label: Business name and contact
        prompt: What is your business name, phone number, and email or WhatsApp?
      - label: Customer name and contact
        prompt: Who is the customer and how will you contact them?
      - label: Date of agreement
        prompt: When was this agreement made?
  - title: Scope of work
    description: Explain exactly what work will be delivered and any important limits.
    rows:
      - label: Service or product
        prompt: What are you providing?
      - label: What is included
        prompt: List the main items, sessions, deliverables, or service stages.
      - label: What is not included
        prompt: Write any items the customer may assume but that are not part of the price.
  - title: Timing and approvals
    description: Set out the timeline and points where the customer must respond or approve.
    rows:
      - label: Start date
        prompt: When will work begin?
      - label: Delivery date
        prompt: When should the final work be ready?
      - label: Approval steps
        prompt: What must the customer approve before the next stage?
  - title: Price and payment terms
    description: Make the money side easy to understand.
    rows:
      - label: Total price
        prompt: What is the full agreed price?
      - label: Deposit or first payment
        prompt: Is any payment due before work starts?
      - label: Final payment terms
        prompt: When is the balance due and by which method?
  - title: Changes, cancellation, and delays
    description: Decide now how changes will be handled.
    rows:
      - label: Customer changes
        prompt: What happens if the customer wants extra work or changes after approval?
      - label: Cancellation terms
        prompt: What happens if the work is cancelled after you have started?
      - label: Delays and communication
        prompt: How will you handle delays and keep the customer updated?
  - title: Confirmation and sign-off
    description: End with a clear record that both sides understand the agreement.
    rows:
      - label: Confirmation statement
        prompt: What short statement confirms that both sides agree?
      - label: Business sign-off
        prompt: Who signs or confirms on behalf of the business?
      - label: Customer sign-off
        prompt: Who signs or confirms on behalf of the customer?
example:
  businessName: Chuma Creative Studio
  businessType: Graphic design and printing support
  summary: A small design service preparing a poster and event flyer package for a community organisation.
  sections:
    - title: Scope and timing
      items:
        - "Work includes one poster, one flyer design, and one round of minor edits."
        - "Start date is Monday and final delivery is Friday afternoon."
        - "Customer must approve the draft by Wednesday evening."
    - title: Price and payment
      items:
        - "Total price is MWK 95,000."
        - "Deposit of MWK 40,000 is paid before design begins."
        - "Remaining balance is paid before print-ready files are sent."
    - title: Changes and sign-off
      items:
        - "Extra edits beyond the agreed round are billed separately."
        - "If the customer pauses feedback for more than 5 days, the timeline moves."
        - "Both sides confirm the agreement by WhatsApp and name."
