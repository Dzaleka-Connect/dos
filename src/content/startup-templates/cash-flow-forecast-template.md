---
title: Cash flow forecast template
shortTitle: Cash flow forecast
description: Estimate the money coming in and going out each month so you can see when cash may be tight and plan before it becomes a crisis.
bestFor: Small businesses handling stock, transport, rent, data, airtime, or other regular monthly costs
estimatedTime: 30 to 40 minutes
intro: Use this template to forecast your monthly cash position. It helps you see whether your business can cover costs, when extra support may be needed, and which months are risky.
order: 3
keywords: cash flow forecast template Dzaleka, small business cash flow Malawi, refugee entrepreneur finance template, Dzaleka business planning tool
whoShouldUse:
  - Businesses selling physical products or buying stock regularly.
  - Service businesses with transport, airtime, data, or staffing costs.
  - Anyone preparing for a loan, grant, or business support application.
beforeYouStart:
  - Collect your recent sales records, notebook entries, or phone money records if you have them.
  - Estimate your normal monthly costs as honestly as possible, including small costs like airtime or transport.
  - Separate business money from personal household spending as much as you can.
sections:
  - title: Opening balance and income
    description: Start with how much cash the business has at the beginning of the month and where income should come from.
    rows:
      - label: Opening balance
        prompt: How much cash is available at the start of the month?
      - label: Expected sales
        prompt: How much do you expect to earn from normal sales?
      - label: Other income
        prompt: Will there be any extra income such as contracts, support, or side services?
  - title: Stock and operating costs
    description: List the main costs that reduce your cash during the month.
    rows:
      - label: Stock or materials
        prompt: How much will you spend on stock, ingredients, materials, or supplies?
      - label: Transport or delivery
        prompt: How much will travel, shipping, or collection cost?
      - label: Rent, data, airtime, or utilities
        prompt: What regular monthly operating costs do you expect?
  - title: People and business support costs
    description: Include wages or informal support payments when they are part of the business.
    rows:
      - label: Staff or helpers
        prompt: Will you pay anyone to help this month?
      - label: Maintenance or repairs
        prompt: Any expected fixing, replacement, or servicing costs?
      - label: Emergency reserve
        prompt: How much do you want to keep untouched if possible?
  - title: Monthly balance
    description: Use this section to see whether the month ends positive or negative.
    rows:
      - label: Total cash in
        prompt: Add opening balance, sales, and other income.
      - label: Total cash out
        prompt: Add all expected costs.
      - label: Closing balance
        prompt: Cash in minus cash out. What will remain at month end?
  - title: Warning signs and action points
    description: Decide early what you will do if cash drops too low.
    rows:
      - label: Low cash warning sign
        prompt: What number tells you cash is becoming unsafe?
      - label: Response action
        prompt: What will you do if that warning sign appears?
      - label: Support option
        prompt: Who can advise or help if cash pressure continues?
example:
  businessName: Tiyende Fresh Bites
  businessType: Cooked food stall
  summary: A food stall owner wants to forecast cash before increasing meal production during a busy period.
  sections:
    - title: Income and costs
      items:
        - "Opening balance: MWK 180,000."
        - "Expected sales: MWK 950,000 from weekday meals and weekend catering trays."
        - "Stock costs: MWK 430,000 for flour, oil, vegetables, charcoal, and packaging."
    - title: Operating costs
      items:
        - "Transport and delivery: MWK 75,000."
        - "Phone/data and airtime: MWK 18,000."
        - "Part-time helper: MWK 120,000."
    - title: Balance and warning sign
      items:
        - "Estimated closing balance: MWK 247,000."
        - "Warning sign: if available cash drops below MWK 80,000 before week 3."
        - "Response: reduce low-profit menu items and delay non-essential purchases."
