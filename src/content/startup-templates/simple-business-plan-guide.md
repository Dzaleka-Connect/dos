---
title: Simple business plan guide
shortTitle: Business plan guide
description: Build a simple business plan covering your vision, customer, offer, market, operations, and money without writing a long consultant-style document.
bestFor: First-time business owners, market traders, service providers, and people preparing to apply for support or funding
estimatedTime: 45 to 60 minutes
intro: Use this guide when you need a stronger overall business plan than a one-page action plan. It helps you explain where the business is going, who it serves, how it will operate, and how it will make money.
order: 6
keywords: business plan guide Dzaleka, simple business plan Malawi, startup business planning refugee entrepreneurs, Dzaleka entrepreneur templates
whoShouldUse:
  - Entrepreneurs starting a new business or formalising one that already exists.
  - Business owners preparing for grants, loans, partnerships, or support programmes.
  - People who need one document that explains the business clearly to others.
beforeYouStart:
  - Decide what your business is trying to achieve over the next 6 to 12 months.
  - Gather basic information about your costs, customers, competitors, and the support you already have.
  - Keep your writing practical and specific. Avoid promises you cannot measure or deliver.
sections:
  - title: Vision and business summary
    description: Start with the short story of the business and the result you want to create.
    rows:
      - label: Business idea
        prompt: What does the business do?
      - label: Vision
        prompt: What do you want the business to become in the next 1 to 3 years?
      - label: Short summary
        prompt: How would you explain the business in a few clear sentences?
  - title: Customer and market
    description: Explain who the business serves and what makes demand real.
    rows:
      - label: Main customer groups
        prompt: Who are your main customers?
      - label: Market need
        prompt: What need, problem, or demand are you responding to?
      - label: Competitors or alternatives
        prompt: Who else provides something similar and how are you different?
  - title: Offer and pricing
    description: Show clearly what you sell and how the business makes money.
    rows:
      - label: Main products or services
        prompt: What are your main offers?
      - label: Unique selling point
        prompt: What makes people choose you?
      - label: Pricing approach
        prompt: How do you price your products or services?
  - title: Operations and delivery
    description: Explain how the business actually works day to day.
    rows:
      - label: Business location or delivery method
        prompt: Where will the business operate and how will customers receive the service or product?
      - label: Equipment, materials, or suppliers
        prompt: What do you need to operate?
      - label: Team and responsibilities
        prompt: Who is involved and what does each person do?
  - title: Marketing and growth
    description: Show how the business will attract customers and build momentum.
    rows:
      - label: Main marketing channels
        prompt: How will people hear about your business?
      - label: Growth goal
        prompt: What growth target do you want over the next 6 to 12 months?
      - label: Partnerships or support
        prompt: Are there schools, NGOs, shops, churches, or community groups that can help?
  - title: Money and next steps
    description: End with the practical money picture and the actions that come next.
    rows:
      - label: Start-up or growth costs
        prompt: What money is needed and for what?
      - label: Income expectation
        prompt: How will the business earn money each month?
      - label: Next actions
        prompt: What are the first 3 things that must happen after this plan is written?
example:
  businessName: Tisunge Learning Hub
  businessType: Tutoring and digital skills support
  summary: A small education business offering after-school tutoring, English support, and beginner digital lessons for young people and adults.
  sections:
    - title: Vision and market
      items:
        - "Vision: become a trusted learning support centre for students and job seekers in Dzaleka."
        - "Main customers are secondary students, exam candidates, and youth seeking basic digital skills."
        - "Need: affordable tutoring and practical skills support outside formal school hours."
    - title: Offer and operations
      items:
        - "Offers include English tutoring, CV support, and computer basics."
        - "Classes run in the evening and on Saturdays using shared laptops and printed materials."
        - "One lead tutor teaches while a second helper manages attendance and follow-up."
    - title: Growth and money
      items:
        - "Marketing uses WhatsApp groups, school contacts, and posters near learning centres."
        - "Growth goal is 40 regular learners within 6 months."
        - "Next steps are to secure a teaching venue, confirm lesson prices, and publish a simple timetable."
