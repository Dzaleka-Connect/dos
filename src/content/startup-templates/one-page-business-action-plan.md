---
title: One-page business action plan
shortTitle: Business action plan
description: Set one business goal, list your top priorities, and break the work into clear actions, deadlines, and responsibilities on one page.
bestFor: People starting a business, restarting after a setback, or trying to organise the next 1 to 3 months
estimatedTime: 20 to 30 minutes
intro: Use this one-page action plan when you want a simple business roadmap that shows what must happen next, who will do it, and what support or money is needed.
order: 2
keywords: business action plan template Dzaleka, startup planning Malawi, one page business plan refugee entrepreneurs, Dzaleka small business template
whoShouldUse:
  - Small businesses that need a short plan for the next few weeks or months.
  - Entrepreneurs preparing to apply for grants, programmes, or marketplace opportunities.
  - People who feel busy but need to focus on the most important actions first.
beforeYouStart:
  - Be clear about your main business goal for the next 1 to 3 months.
  - Write down your current situation honestly, including any challenges around stock, cash, equipment, or customers.
  - Decide who is helping you, even if it is only one family member, partner, or assistant.
sections:
  - title: Goal and current situation
    description: Start with one clear target and a short view of where the business stands now.
    rows:
      - label: Main goal
        prompt: What result do you want by the end of this plan period?
      - label: Current situation
        prompt: What is happening in the business right now?
      - label: Main challenge
        prompt: What is the biggest thing slowing progress?
  - title: Top three priorities
    description: Choose the three priorities that matter most right now.
    rows:
      - label: Priority 1
        prompt: What must happen first?
      - label: Priority 2
        prompt: What is next after that?
      - label: Priority 3
        prompt: What else is important in this period?
  - title: Actions
    description: Break each priority into practical tasks.
    rows:
      - label: Action steps
        prompt: List the actions you must complete for each priority.
      - label: Who is responsible
        prompt: Who will do each action?
      - label: Timeline
        prompt: When will each action be done?
  - title: Resources needed
    description: List what you need so you can spot gaps early.
    rows:
      - label: Materials or tools
        prompt: What equipment, materials, or stock do you need?
      - label: Money needed
        prompt: How much cash is required and for what purpose?
      - label: Outside support
        prompt: Do you need training, approval, transport, or a partner?
  - title: Review and follow-up
    description: Decide how you will check progress and adjust the plan.
    rows:
      - label: Review date
        prompt: When will you review this plan?
      - label: Success signs
        prompt: What signs will show you are on track?
      - label: Adjustment point
        prompt: What will you change if progress is too slow?
example:
  businessName: Zikomo Mobile Services
  businessType: Mobile money, airtime, and phone charging kiosk
  summary: A kiosk owner wants to grow daily customer numbers and reduce days when float runs too low.
  sections:
    - title: Goal and priorities
      items:
        - "Goal: increase daily transactions by 20 percent within 8 weeks."
        - "Priority 1: improve float planning for busy days."
        - "Priority 2: make the kiosk easier to find and trust."
    - title: Actions
      items:
        - "Track daily float use for two weeks."
        - "Paint a clear service sign and display hours."
        - "Offer a simple customer reminder on WhatsApp for weekend availability."
    - title: Resources and review
      items:
        - "Need MWK 35,000 for sign updates and extra charging cable stock."
        - "Need one family member to cover lunch hours on market days."
        - "Review after 4 weeks using daily transaction records."
