---
title: Marketing plan template
shortTitle: Marketing plan
description: Plan how you will reach customers, explain what you sell, and decide which low-cost channels to use each week and each month.
bestFor: Market traders, food stalls, tailoring, beauty services, crafts, and small digital services
estimatedTime: 30 to 45 minutes
intro: Use this template to decide who your customers are, what you want them to know, and how you will reach them without spending more than your business can manage.
order: 1
keywords: marketing plan template Dzaleka, small business marketing Malawi, startup promotion template refugee entrepreneurs, Dzaleka business planning
whoShouldUse:
  - New business owners who are starting to promote a product or service.
  - Small businesses that rely on repeat local customers and referrals.
  - Traders who want a simple weekly and monthly promotion routine.
beforeYouStart:
  - List the products or services you want to focus on first.
  - "Think about your main customer groups: camp residents, host community buyers, organisations, or online customers."
  - Check what promotion methods you can realistically manage, such as WhatsApp, flyers, word of mouth, market display, or Facebook.
sections:
  - title: Customer groups
    description: Write down the customer groups you most want to reach first.
    rows:
      - label: Main customer group
        prompt: Who is most likely to buy from you regularly?
      - label: Secondary customer group
        prompt: Who else may buy from you sometimes?
      - label: Customer need
        prompt: What problem are you solving or what need are you meeting?
  - title: Products and services
    description: Keep this focused on what you want to promote now, not everything you might do later.
    rows:
      - label: Main product or service
        prompt: What are you promoting first?
      - label: Key benefit
        prompt: Why would someone choose you instead of another seller?
      - label: Most profitable offer
        prompt: Which product or service helps your business most?
  - title: Pricing
    description: Use this section to make sure your pricing matches what customers can understand and compare.
    rows:
      - label: Price point
        prompt: What price will you advertise publicly?
      - label: Special offer
        prompt: Will you run bundles, discounts, or loyalty offers?
      - label: Reason for this price
        prompt: Why is this price fair for you and the customer?
  - title: Channels
    description: Choose channels you can use consistently, even with a small budget.
    rows:
      - label: In-person channels
        prompt: Market stall, posters, church, school, events, shop signs, or referrals?
      - label: Digital channels
        prompt: WhatsApp status, Facebook, phone calls, SMS, or a marketplace listing?
      - label: Best channel
        prompt: Which one brings the most serious customers?
  - title: Weekly and monthly promotion plan
    description: Break promotion into small actions you can actually keep doing.
    rows:
      - label: Weekly actions
        prompt: What will you do every week to stay visible?
      - label: Monthly actions
        prompt: What bigger action will you do once each month?
      - label: Help needed
        prompt: Who can help with photos, wording, printing, or posting?
  - title: Budget
    description: Keep promotion costs realistic and linked to your expected return.
    rows:
      - label: Promotion budget
        prompt: How much can you spend each month?
      - label: Main costs
        prompt: Printing, transport, data, airtime, signage, or samples?
      - label: Low-cost options
        prompt: What can you do for free or almost free?
  - title: Success measures
    description: Choose a few signs that tell you whether the plan is working.
    rows:
      - label: Sales target
        prompt: What sales result do you want this month?
      - label: Customer target
        prompt: How many enquiries or new customers do you want?
      - label: Review date
        prompt: When will you check what is working and what is not?
example:
  businessName: Bright Thread Tailoring
  businessType: Tailoring and clothing repairs
  summary: A small tailoring business serving school families, church groups, and market customers in and around Dzaleka.
  sections:
    - title: Customer groups
      items:
        - "Main group: parents needing school uniform repairs and adjustments."
        - "Secondary group: women ordering dresses for church, weddings, and community events."
        - "Need: reliable tailoring, fast repairs, and neat finishing."
    - title: Channels and promotion
      items:
        - "Weekly actions: post finished work on WhatsApp status every Monday and Friday."
        - "Monthly action: visit one church women’s group and one school contact with sample photos."
        - "Best channel: referrals from satisfied customers and WhatsApp photos."
    - title: Budget and success measures
      items:
        - "Budget: MWK 20,000 per month for data, airtime, and two poster updates."
        - "Sales target: 25 repair jobs and 6 custom clothing orders this month."
        - "Review date: last Saturday of each month."
