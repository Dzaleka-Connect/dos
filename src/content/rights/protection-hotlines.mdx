## Understanding These Services

### 📞 National Toll-Free Numbers
These numbers are **free to call** from any Malawian SIM card (Airtel or TNM). They are your first line of defense.
*   **5600 (Tithandizane)**: The primary line for Gender-Based Violence (GBV), child marriage, and human trafficking.
*   **116 (Child Protection)**: Specifically for reporting abuse or exploitation of children.
*   **393 (Youth)**: A confidential space for young people to discuss relationships and sexual health.

### 🇺🇳 UNHCR Contacts
*   **Main Line**: General inquiries.
*   **Email (mlwli@unhcr.org)**: Use this for detailed, written protection inquiries or documentation of status concerns where a paper trail is important.
