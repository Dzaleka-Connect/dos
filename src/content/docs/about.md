# About Dzaleka Online Services

Your digital gateway to Dzaleka Refugee Camp services, resources, and community connections.

## Our Mission

Dzaleka Online Services is dedicated to:
- Connecting refugees with 100+ verified service organizations
- Providing access to 500+ educational and policy resources
- Supporting 57,000+ refugees in Malawi's largest settlement
- Bridging the 79% humanitarian funding gap through information access
- Empowering refugee-led initiatives and organizations
- Facilitating community connections and support networks

## Platform Features

### Service Directory
- **100+ Verified Organizations**: Healthcare, education, legal support, cultural programs
- **Contact Information**: Direct access to service providers
- **Category Navigation**: Easy browsing by service type
- **Verified Badges**: Quality assurance for trusted services
- **Map Integration**: Location-based service discovery

### Resources Library
- **500+ Documents**: Legal guides, policy documents, research reports
- **Multi-language Support**: English, Chichewa, French, and more
- **Educational Materials**: Training guides, templates, and toolkits
- **Research Access**: Academic studies and refugee rights documentation
- **Downloadable Content**: PDF resources for offline access

### Events Platform
- **Community Calendar**: Workshops, festivals, conferences, and gatherings
- **Event Details**: Date, location, registration information, panelist profiles
- **Past Events**: Access to historical event records
- **Event Submission**: Organizations can list their events
- **Categories**: Cultural, educational, advocacy, and more

### Job Board
- **Employment Listings**: Full-time, part-time, contract, volunteer positions
- **Multiple Categories**: Education, healthcare, technology, arts, community work
- **Application Details**: Direct contact with hiring organizations
- **Deadlines Tracking**: Stay updated on application timelines
- **Skills Matching**: Find opportunities that match your expertise

### Photo Gallery
- **Community Documentation**: Visual stories of life in Dzaleka
- **Photographer Profiles**: Credit and connect with photographers
- **Year-based Collections**: Browse photos by time period
- **Photo Submission**: Share your community documentation
- **Cultural Heritage**: Preserve visual history

### Community Voice
- **Personal Stories**: Share experiences and narratives
- **Success Stories**: Highlight achievements and resilience
- **Cultural Stories**: Document traditions and heritage
- **Community Connection**: Engage with fellow community members
- **Voice Submission**: Platform for refugee voices

## Our Impact

### By the Numbers
- **100+ Service Organizations**: Verified and accessible
- **500+ Resources**: Educational and policy documents
- **57,000+ People Served**: Refugees in Dzaleka and surrounding areas
- **30+ Events Monthly**: Community activities and programs
- **Multiple Languages**: Accessible to diverse communities

### Who We Serve
- **Refugees in Dzaleka Camp**: Primary beneficiaries seeking services
- **Service Organizations**: NGOs, CBOs, and refugee-led initiatives
- **Researchers & Advocates**: Access to data and documentation
- **Host Community**: Local Malawians engaging with services
- **International Partners**: Organizations supporting refugee populations
