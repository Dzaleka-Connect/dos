# Platform Features

Discover the comprehensive features of **Dzaleka Online Services** - your digital gateway to refugee services, resources, and community connections in Malawi's largest refugee settlement.

## Core Features

### 🏢 Service Directory

**100+ Verified Organizations at Your Fingertips**

- **Comprehensive Search**: Find services by name, category, or keyword
- **10+ Categories**: Education, Healthcare, Legal Support, Cultural Programs, Humanitarian Aid, Youth Services, Entrepreneurship, Advocacy, and more
- **Verified Services**: Quality assurance with verification badges
- **Featured Services**: Highlighted organizations with exceptional impact
- **Detailed Profiles**: Contact information, hours, location, social media
- **Map Integration**: Location-based service discovery
- **Direct Contact**: Email, phone, WhatsApp, and website links

**Service Categories:**
- Education & Training
- Healthcare & Wellness
- Legal Support & Advocacy
- Humanitarian Aid
- Cultural & Arts Programs
- Youth & Children Services
- Entrepreneurship & Business Support
- Community Development
- Emergency Response
- Skills Development

### 📚 Resources Library

**500+ Documents for Education, Advocacy, and Support**

- **Extensive Collection**: Legal guides, policy documents, research reports, educational materials
- **Multi-Format Support**: PDF, DOCX, videos, links, audio resources
- **Language Diversity**: English, Chichewa, French, Swahili, and more
- **Category Navigation**: Easy browsing by resource type
- **Search & Filter**: Find exactly what you need
- **Download Capabilities**: Access resources offline
- **Author Attribution**: Credit to creators and organizations
- **Date Tracking**: Stay updated with latest versions

**Resource Categories:**
- Legal Documents & Policies
- Educational Materials
- Research & Reports
- Guides & Toolkits
- Training Materials
- Advocacy Resources
- Community Templates
- Health Information
- Business Resources
- Cultural Documentation

### 📅 Events Calendar

**Stay Connected with 30+ Monthly Community Activities**

- **Upcoming Events**: Workshops, training, festivals, conferences
- **Past Events Archive**: Access historical event information
- **Event Details**: Date, time, location, description, registration links
- **Panelist Profiles**: Learn about speakers and organizers
- **Event Categories**: Cultural, educational, advocacy, community, arts
- **Registration Integration**: Direct links to event registration
- **Event Submission**: Organizations can list their events
- **Featured Events**: Highlighted important community gatherings
- **Calendar View**: See all events at a glance

### 💼 Job Board

**Employment and Volunteer Opportunities**

- **Job Listings**: Full-time, part-time, contract, volunteer, internship positions
- **Multiple Categories**: Education, healthcare, technology, arts, community work, administration
- **Application Details**: Salary information, requirements, deadlines
- **Organization Profiles**: Learn about hiring organizations
- **Skills Matching**: Find opportunities that match your expertise
- **Status Tracking**: Open and closed positions clearly marked
- **Featured Jobs**: Highlighted opportunities
- **Direct Applications**: Contact information for applying
- **Deadline Alerts**: Stay informed about application timelines

### 📸 Photo Gallery

**Visual Documentation of Community Life**

- **35+ Curated Photos**: Community life, events, culture, people
- **Photographer Attribution**: Credit and connect with photographers
- **Year Collections**: Browse photos by 2023, 2024, 2025
- **Photo Stories**: Each image tells a community story
- **Location Tagging**: Where photos were taken
- **Tags & Categories**: Easy navigation
- **Photo Submission**: Share your visual documentation
- **Featured Photos**: Highlighted impactful images
- **High-Quality Images**: Professional and community photography

### 🎤 Community Voice

**Platform for Refugee Stories and Voices**

- **Personal Narratives**: Share your experiences and journey
- **Success Stories**: Highlight achievements and resilience
- **Cultural Stories**: Document traditions and heritage
- **Community Insights**: Perspectives from camp life
- **Author Profiles**: Connect with storytellers
- **Featured Stories**: Highlighted compelling narratives
- **Story Submission**: Easy platform for sharing
- **Categories**: Personal, Cultural, Community, Success
- **Engagement**: Read and connect with community members

### 🤝 Skills Exchange (Coming Soon)

**Connect, Learn, and Grow Together**

- **Talent Directory**: Find skilled community members
- **Skill Categories**: Arts, technology, education, trades, languages
- **Service Listings**: Paid and free services
- **Profile Creation**: Showcase your skills and expertise
- **Direct Contact**: Connect with service providers
- **Rate Information**: Transparent pricing
- **Reviews & Endorsements**: Community validation

## Interactive Features

### 🔍 Advanced Search

**Find What You Need Quickly**

- **Full-Text Search**: Search across all content
- **Multi-Collection Search**: Services, events, resources, news, jobs, photos
- **Smart Filters**: Category, language, date, type
- **Search Suggestions**: Auto-complete and recommendations
- **Recent Searches**: Quick access to previous searches
- **Search Results**: Organized by content type
- **Server-Side Processing**: Fast results even with 500+ items
- **Caching**: Lightning-fast repeated searches

### 📊 Analytics & Insights

**Data-Driven Community Information**

- **Service Statistics**: Number of organizations by category
- **Resource Metrics**: Document counts and access trends
- **Event Tracking**: Participation and attendance data
- **Community Demographics**: Population statistics
- **Usage Analytics**: Platform engagement metrics
- **Impact Reporting**: Visualize community support

### 🌐 API Access

**Developer-Friendly Integration**

- **RESTful API**: Access all data programmatically
- **Multiple Endpoints**: Services, events, resources, news, photos, jobs
- **JSON Responses**: Standard format for easy integration
- **CORS Support**: Cross-origin requests enabled
- **Rate Limiting**: 60 requests per minute
- **API Documentation**: Complete reference at /api-docs
- **Search API**: Server-side search with caching
- **No Authentication Required**: Public access to data

## Platform Capabilities

### 📱 Responsive Design

- **Mobile-First**: Optimized for phones and tablets
- **Desktop Support**: Full-featured experience on computers
- **Touch-Friendly**: Easy navigation on all devices
- **Adaptive Layouts**: Content adjusts to screen size
- **Fast Loading**: Optimized performance

### 🌍 Multi-Language Support

- **Content in Multiple Languages**: English, Chichewa, French, Swahili
- **Language Tags**: Resources marked by language
- **Inclusive Access**: Serving diverse linguistic communities
- **Translation Ready**: Infrastructure for adding more languages

### ♿ Accessibility

- **Screen Reader Support**: ARIA labels and semantic HTML
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Full site access without mouse
- **High Contrast**: Readable color schemes
- **Alt Text**: Image descriptions for visually impaired
- **Focus Indicators**: Clear navigation paths

### 🚀 Performance Optimizations

- **Image Optimization**: Cloudinary CDN with auto-format (WebP/AVIF)
- **Lazy Loading**: Images load as you scroll
- **Search Caching**: 5-minute cache for fast repeated queries
- **Rate Limiting**: Prevent abuse and ensure availability
- **CDN Delivery**: Fast content delivery globally
- **Static Generation**: Pre-rendered pages for speed

### 🔒 Privacy & Security

- **Privacy Policy**: Clear data usage guidelines
- **No Unnecessary Tracking**: Minimal analytics, no third-party trackers
- **Secure Forms**: HTTPS encryption for all submissions
- **Content Moderation**: Quality control for submissions
- **CORS Protection**: Configured cross-origin policies
