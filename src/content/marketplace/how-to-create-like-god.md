## About the book

*How To Create Like God* uses the Genesis creation narrative as a framework for creative work and entrepreneurship. The book discusses communication, personal branding, productivity, and developing ideas through a Christian perspective.

The independently published paperback appeared on 21 August 2023. It is 144 pages and carries ISBN 9798858414087. An unabridged audiobook was released in May 2024.

James Niyomugabo spent about a decade in Dzaleka before resettling in Canada. This book is a work by a former resident; it is not a memoir or a history of the camp.
