## About the Single

**"Mapulani (Dzaleka Version)"** is a special collaboration between Malawian artist **Namadingo** and **Israel Kabongo**, a talented musician and refugee from Dzaleka. 

This version of the hit song "Mapulani" includes a verse translated into Swahili, representing the linguistic diversity of the camp. It was produced after Namadingo visited the camp and was inspired by Israel's talent, marking a significant moment of artistic collaboration between the host community and refugees.
