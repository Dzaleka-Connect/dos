## About the book

*Run Elvin!* records Gabriel Ndayishimiye's childhood and youth in Dzaleka Refugee Camp. Written partly as a letter from a father to his son, it considers genocide, forced movement, camp life, education, resettlement policy, and the work of preserving family memory.

The revised 2025 paperback is 166 pages and carries ISBN 9781778286308. Earlier paperback and ebook editions were published by Laskin Publishing.
