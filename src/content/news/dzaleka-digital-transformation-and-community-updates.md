## 🚀 Starlink Brings Reliable Internet to Dzaleka

A transformative development has arrived at Dzaleka Refugee Camp with the introduction of Starlink's satellite internet service. This initiative provides residents with stable and fast internet connectivity, enabling access to online education, healthcare information, and global communication tools. For many in the camp, this marks the first time they can engage with the digital world in such a meaningful way.

## 📚 Empowering Through Practical Knowledge

The recently published Malawi Refugee Guide offers comprehensive insights into life at Dzaleka. It covers the camp's history, legal frameworks affecting refugees, and daily living conditions. ## ⚠️ Urgent Appeal: Food Assistance at Risk

The World Food Program (WFP) has raised concerns over a potential suspension of food assistance at Dzaleka due to funding shortages. The agency requires approximately $10 million USD to maintain its food support operations. This situation underscores the critical need for continued support and funding to ensure food security for the camp's residents.

[Source: Dzaleka Faces Food Crisis as WFP Warns of Possible Suspension of Food Assistance](https://www.dzaleka.com/2025/04/dzaleka-faces-food-crisis-as-wfp-warns.html)

## 🧠 Mental Health: Addressing the Unseen Struggles

A recent study highlights the prevalence of depression and suicidal tendencies among individuals living in Dzaleka Refugee Camp. The research emphasizes the importance of mental health support and the need for comprehensive services to address these challenges.

[Source: It is time to think about refugees' mental health: The case of Dzaleka Refugee Camp - Malawi](https://services.dzaleka.com/resources/it_is_time_to_think_about_refugees_mental_health_the_case_of_dzaleka_refugee_camp_-_malawi/)
