Dzaleka Marketplace is here.

We built this platform to make it easier for people in our community to sell what they make and offer, and for buyers to find them. Whether you're looking for handmade crafts, need a tutor, want to order food, or just want to see what's available locally - it's all in one place now.

## What's on the Marketplace?

**Products** - Crafts, clothing, books, art, electronics, and other goods from local vendors.

**Services** - Tutoring, repairs, photography, tailoring, and other skilled work.

**Restaurants & Shops** - A separate section for eateries, cafes, bakeries, and retail stores. You can see their menus, hours, and how to contact them.

## Books by Local Authors

We're also featuring books written by authors from our community:

- **[Refugee for Life](/marketplace/refugee-for-life-book)** by Innocent Magambi
- **[Being a Refugee Wasn't a Choice](/marketplace/being-a-refugee-wasnt-a-choice)** - poetry anthology by Charles Lipanda Mahigwe and others

More titles coming soon.

## For Sellers

Listing is free. No fees, no commissions. **100% goes to you.** Customers contact you directly through WhatsApp or phone - no middleman involved.

If you run a restaurant, cafe, or shop, you can register your business to show up in the Restaurants & Shops section with your full menu and details.

## Why It Matters

When you buy from Dzaleka Marketplace, your money goes directly to someone in the community. Every purchase supports a local entrepreneur trying to build something.
