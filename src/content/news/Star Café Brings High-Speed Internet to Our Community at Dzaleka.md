Star Café has officially launched a high-speed internet café in our community at Dzaleka Refugee Camp, offering blazing-fast Starlink Mini service at over 125 Mbps for just 200MK per hour. Open Monday to Saturday from 8AM to 7PM, the café provides seamless streaming, lag-free gaming, and a reliable space for study and remote work. Visit Star Café at Kawale 2, opposite the Borehole.
