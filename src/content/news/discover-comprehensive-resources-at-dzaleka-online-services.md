# Discover Comprehensive Resources at Dzaleka Online Services

At [Dzaleka Online Services](https://services.dzaleka.com), we're proud to offer an extensive resource library that serves as a comprehensive knowledge hub for our community. Our [Resources section](/resources) contains a wealth of materials ranging from community initiatives to international legal documents, all designed to support learning, advocacy, and community development.

## What You'll Find in Our Resource Library

Our resource collection is organized into several key categories, each serving different needs and interests:

### 📊 **Research and Reports**

Discover in-depth studies and analyses that provide valuable insights into life in Dzaleka and refugee communities:

- **[Community Mapping in Dzaleka Refugee Camp](/resources/dzaleka-community-mapping-2025)** - A detailed case study examining participatory mapping initiatives and their implementation challenges
- **[Protection Monitoring Survey - Dzaleka Refugee Camp 2023](/resources/dzaleka-protection-monitoring-2023)** - Regular monitoring assessment of protection needs and risks faced by refugees
- **[Dzaleka Socioeconomic Assessment 2017](/resources/dzaleka-socioeconomic-assessment-2017)** - Comprehensive analysis of economic conditions and opportunities
- **[Dzaleka Livelihoods Survey 2022](/resources/dzaleka-livelihoods-survey-2022)** - Latest data on employment and income-generating activities

### 📋 **Situation Reports and Updates**

Stay informed with current situation reports and operational updates:

- **[DRC Situation Update 16 - 2025](/resources/drc-situation-update-16-2025)** - Latest developments affecting Congolese refugees
- **[UNHCR Southern Africa Operational Update - March 2025](/resources/unhcr-southern-africa-operational-update-march-2025)** - Regional operational overview
- **[Malawi Funding Update 2025](/resources/malawi-funding-update-2025)** - Current funding situation and needs
- **[Dzaleka Cash Intervention 2020](/resources/dzaleka-cash-intervention-2020)** - Analysis of cash assistance programs
