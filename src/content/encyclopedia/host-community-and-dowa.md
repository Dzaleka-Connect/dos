## Location

Dzaleka is in Dowa District in central Malawi, about 41 kilometres from the capital, Lilongwe. The camp occupies the grounds of a former prison and sits among the hills of the district, surrounded by Malawian villages.

## The host community

The Malawian population living around the camp relies largely on subsistence farming. Aid organisations working at Dzaleka describe the surrounding area as historically underserved, with residents facing constraints such as limited farming inputs, unstable crop prices, and variable rainfall. This means the camp's needs sit alongside those of a host population with its own poverty.

## Shared programmes

Several organisations working at Dzaleka extend their programmes to host-community residents rather than serving refugees alone. [There is Hope](/encyclopedia/there-is-hope-malawi), a Malawian NGO, describes its education and livelihoods work as covering both refugees and the Dowa host community, and its social enterprise [Kibébé](/encyclopedia/kibebe) employs people from both groups. Sports and cultural events documented elsewhere in this encyclopedia also bring the two communities together.
