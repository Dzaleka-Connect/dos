## Overview

**Amisi Hassan Jospin** (also known as **Jospin Amisi Hassan**) is a Congolese refugee software engineer, artificial intelligence researcher, and educator based in Malawi. He is an AI researcher at **[Rexplore Research Labs](/encyclopedia/rexplore-labs)**, an **MIT Emerging Talent fellow**, and co-founded **[ADAI Circle](/encyclopedia/adai-circle)** on 2 February 2020 alongside **[Byamasu Patrick Paul](/encyclopedia/byamasu-patrick-paul)**.

---

## Technical Contributions & Research

### 1. Research at Rexplore Research Labs
At **Rexplore Research Labs**, Jospin contributes to machine learning models, dataset curation, and natural language processing (NLP) focusing on low-resource African languages. His open-source machine learning contributions on Hugging Face and GitHub support reinforcement learning and voice AI architectures built for African contexts.

### 2. MIT Emerging Talent & Academic Fellowships
Jospin completed the **MIT Emerging Talent programme**, using his platform to connect Dzaleka youth with global AI education:
* **MIT RAISE Partnership**: Co-designed and piloted the *Responsible AI for Social Empowerment and Education (RAICA)* curriculum inside Dzaleka, teaching students natural language processing and chatbot architecture.
* **MIT Bootcamps**: Adapted rapid prototyping and hackathon methodologies for ADAI Circle students.

### 3. Internet Society (ISOC) Certifications
Jospin has engaged with the **Internet Society (ISOC)**, completing certifications in technical internet governance and digital security, including coursework on encryption threats and open internet advocacy.

---

## Community Leadership

Through ADAI Circle and Rexplore Research Labs, Amisi Hassan Jospin has helped cultivate a culture of advanced software development and artificial intelligence research among displaced youth in Malawi.
