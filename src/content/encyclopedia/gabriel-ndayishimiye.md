## Early life

Gabriel Ndayishimiye fled his home during the 1994 Rwandan genocide and grew up as a refugee in Dzaleka. He has described his path as a journey "of healing and self-discovery, from a refugee camp in Malawi to a new life halfway around the world."

## Writing and publishing

Ndayishimiye is the author of the memoir [*Run Elvin!*](/encyclopedia/run-elvin-book), which draws on his childhood and youth in the camp. He founded LivenBooks, a publisher he set up to reach the growing number of refugee and immigrant writers documenting displacement and its histories.

## Later work

Reporting from 2021 and 2022 placed Ndayishimiye in London and as a graduate student in immigration and settlement studies at Ryerson University (now Toronto Metropolitan University) in Canada, where he took part in a peer-mentoring programme for displaced scholars. He is one of several Dzaleka writers, alongside [Oliver Twist](/encyclopedia/oliver-twist) and [Njamba Koffi](/encyclopedia/njamba-koffi), who have published memoirs of camp life.
