## Origins

Fountain of Hope Africa was founded by [Marcel Cirhuza](/encyclopedia/marcel-cirhuza), who fled insecurity in eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo and lived in Dzaleka for several years. The organisation describes itself as a refugee-led organisation registered in Malawi, run from a base in Dowa, near the camp.

## Activities

The organisation provides emergency assistance, including food, shelter, healthcare, clean water, and cash support, along with psychosocial services, working with refugees, internally displaced people, and members of the host community. Its stated area of work spans Dzaleka and eastern DRC. It has been named as a local partner in football-based mental health activities run in the camp with outside foundations.

## Context

Fountain of Hope Africa is one of several organisations started by former or current Dzaleka residents, alongside [Inua Advocacy](/encyclopedia/inua-advocacy) and [There is Hope](/encyclopedia/there-is-hope-malawi), which are documented in this encyclopedia's [overview of refugee-led organisations](/encyclopedia/refugee-led-organisations).
