## Story and audience

The book follows a child whose family is forced to leave home because of violence. After reaching Malawi, the family lives in Dzaleka, where restricted work opportunities and severe material hardship shape daily life. Their involvement with the Nação Ubuntu project changes the opportunities available to the family.

The publisher classifies the 80-page work as children's and young-adult literature. Vanessa Alexandre is credited as illustrator and as the person whose experience inspired the story.

## Editions and titles

The documented Portuguese edition is *Nação Ubuntu: Um Lugar Chamado Esperança*, published by Editora Chico Xavier in 2023 with ISBN 9786589497622. The marketplace lists an English-language cover titled *Ubuntu Nation: A Place Called Hope* and links to an English Amazon edition.

These are edition titles of the same work, not separate books. The encyclopedia uses the bibliographically documented Portuguese title and records the English title as an alias.

## Dzaleka in the narrative

Dzaleka is a named setting rather than background context. The book introduces younger readers to forced migration, encampment, poverty, sponsorship, and organised support through one family's story. Its account is written from the perspective of the Nação Ubuntu initiative, so claims about the project's impact should be read as part of that organisational context rather than as an independent evaluation.
