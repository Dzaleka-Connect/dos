## Displacement and testimony

Joseph Ndereyimana fled Rwanda during the 1994 genocide and, after a journey across the region, reached Dzaleka in 1995, the year after the camp was established. His first-person account of that flight and arrival was published in 1999 as [*Walk This Way: The Journey of a Rwandan Refugee*](/encyclopedia/walk-this-way-book), told to Carolyn Neville and edited by Janet Jackson.

## Context

*Walk This Way* is one of the earliest published memoirs tied to Dzaleka, describing the camp in its first years. It sits alongside later camp memoirs by writers such as [Gabriel Ndayishimiye](/encyclopedia/gabriel-ndayishimiye), documented elsewhere in this encyclopedia.
