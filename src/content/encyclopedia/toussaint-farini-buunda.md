## Salama Africa

Toussaint Farini Buunda is a Congolese community organiser associated with Salama Africa, a youth organisation established in Dzaleka in 2014. In an oral-history profile, Farini described starting the organisation with Fred Farini and Alain Tenta as a platform for young artists.

Salama Africa developed activities in dance, music, visual art, and tailoring. Jesuit Refugee Service reported on its youth arts work in 2017, while a 2023 profile by Imagine Worldwide identified Farini as the organisation's director.

## Education programmes

In 2023, Salama Africa partnered with Imagine Worldwide to offer tablet-based literacy and numeracy lessons for children waiting for places in formal schools. Imagine Worldwide reported that the programme was designed for 1,000 learners using 200 tablets.

Farini's public work connects creative practice with education and community organising. Programme figures and roles in this entry are tied to the dates of the cited reports and are not presented as a current service directory.
