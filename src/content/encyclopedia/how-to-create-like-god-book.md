## Work and editions

*How To Create Like God* presents James Niyomugabo's reading of the Genesis creation narrative as a framework for creative work, personal branding, communication, productivity, and entrepreneurship. It is a religious and practical guide, not a memoir.

The independently published paperback appeared on 21 August 2023. Bookshop.org records 144 pages and ISBN 9798858414087. An unabridged audiobook followed on 22 May 2024. The subtitle and wording of the title vary slightly between catalogues, so those forms are retained as aliases for search.

## Dzaleka connection

Niyomugabo is connected to this encyclopedia as a former Dzaleka resident. The Dzaleka Connect community directory says he spent about a decade in the camp, while Audible's author biography records that part of his education took place there before he later lived in South Africa and Canada.

This distinction matters: the book was written by a person connected to Dzaleka, but its subject is creativity and entrepreneurship through a Christian framework. It should not be cited as a source about the history or conditions of the camp.

## Later work

Niyomugabo has continued the book's ideas through the Create Like God podcast, courses, and digital materials. Those later products are separate from the 2023 paperback and 2024 audiobook documented here.
