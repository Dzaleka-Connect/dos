## A second generation of tech education

ADAI Circle — Africa Deep Artificial Intelligence Circle — was established in Dzaleka on 2 February 2020 by Byamasu Patrick Paul and Amisi Hassan Jospin. Where the camp's earlier coding initiatives focused on general programming, ADAI Circle concentrates on artificial intelligence and data science, alongside software development and hardware design.

Its innovation hub also offers internet access to anyone in need — a significant service in a camp where connectivity is scarce and expensive.

## MIT connections

Co-founder Amisi Hassan Jospin's route ran through MIT's Emerging Talent programme, and the organisation has adopted the MIT Bootcamps model for its annual hackathon. In 2023, ADAI Circle connected with MIT's Responsible AI for Social Empowerment and Education (RAISE) initiative, piloting the Responsible AI for Computational Action curriculum with students in the camp — covering skills such as chatbots and natural language processing.

## Context

ADAI Circle belongs to a lineage of refugee-founded technology education in Dzaleka that includes TakeNoLab and the Dzaleka AppFactory. Together these groups have made the camp an unusual concentration of grassroots tech training, run largely by people who learned their skills inside it.
