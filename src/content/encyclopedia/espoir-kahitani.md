## Poetry

Espoir Kahitani is a spoken-word poet in Dzaleka. [Africa.com](https://africa.com/we-name-ourselves-poetry-as-resistance-in-one-of-africas-largest-refugee-camps/)'s coverage of the camp's poetry scene, reported around the release of [*We Name Ourselves*](/encyclopedia/we-name-ourselves-film), described him at nineteen as one of the most recognised performers in the camp.

He is among the featured poets in *We Name Ourselves*, the HOME Storytellers documentary that follows young Dzaleka poets preparing to perform at the [Tumaini Festival](/encyclopedia/tumaini-festival). The film has its own entry in this encyclopedia.

## Context

Kahitani is part of a spoken-word scene in the camp documented by [OkayAfrica](https://www.okayafrica.com/we-are-the-dream-diggers-poetry-becomes-a-lifeline-in-malawis-dzaleka-refugee-camp/243334) and other outlets, in which young poets perform in courtyards, classrooms, and at festivals. Reporting connects the scene to the camp's best-known poet, [Menes la Plume](/encyclopedia/menes-la-plume), whose festival provided a public platform for performers.
