## Dzaleka Youth in Action

Divine Irakoze was born in a refugee camp in Tanzania to Burundian parents and moved with her family to Malawi in 2004. She grew up in Dzaleka and became involved in youth organising and refugee-rights advocacy.

A 2021 interview identified Irakoze as the founder and team leader of Dzaleka Youth in Action, a youth-led initiative supporting vulnerable children, young people, and older residents. The group organised household assistance during the COVID-19 period and advocated for greater access to education, work, and movement.

## Writing and international advocacy

Irakoze contributed writing and interviews to the Dzaleka Art Project, including accounts of growing up under encampment restrictions and leaving Dzaleka to study international relations at Brigham Young University-Idaho in 2022.

In April 2025, she spoke at the United Nations Economic and Social Council Youth Forum in New York. BYU-Idaho's student newspaper reported that she used the forum to discuss refugee education and the contribution refugees can make when they have access to opportunity.
