## Work

Primo Luanda Bauma is a Congolese photographer and filmmaker living in Dzaleka, who fled the Democratic Republic of the Congo. He leads the photography team for the [Dzaleka Art Project](/encyclopedia/dzaleka-art-project) and has contributed photographs used across the project, including portraits of other camp artists.

## Teaching

Bauma runs free photography classes in the camp. By his account, he began the classes to give young people something to do, a means of artistic expression, and training that could help them earn an income.
