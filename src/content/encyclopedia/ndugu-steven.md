## Writing

Ndugu Steven is a young writer living as a refugee in Dzaleka who publishes poetry, short stories, essays, and journalism. He writes on [The GOAT PoL](https://thegoatpol.org/author/ndugusteve692/), a platform that publishes work by writers in precarious situations, and has described his aim as using writing to contribute to change.

## Studies

By his own account, Steven grew up in a household of eight children where money was scarce, and he studies business and computer science with [Jesuit Worldwide Learning](/encyclopedia/jesuit-worldwide-learning), the higher-education programme that operates a centre in the camp.
