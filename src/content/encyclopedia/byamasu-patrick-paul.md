## Overview

**Byamasu Patrick Paul** is a refugee technology innovator, artificial intelligence researcher, and entrepreneur. He is the **CEO and Chief Research Scientist at [Rexplore Research Labs](/encyclopedia/rexplore-labs)** in Lilongwe, Malawi, and co-founded **[ADAI Circle](/encyclopedia/adai-circle)** in Dzaleka Refugee Camp on 2 February 2020 alongside **[Amisi Hassan Jospin](/encyclopedia/amisi-hassan-jospin)**.

---

## Executive & Research Leadership

### 1. CEO & Chief Research Scientist at Rexplore Research Labs
As CEO and Chief Research Scientist at **Rexplore Research Labs** (`rexplore.ai`), Byamasu Patrick Paul directs research in:
* **Reinforcement Learning & Autonomous Agents**: Designing multi-agent decision systems for cloud automation and market analytics.
* **Low-Resource Language Processing**: Building voice AI and speech models tailored for African languages and constrained infrastructure.
* **Luso8 Cloud**: Leading product architecture for Luso8 Cloud, an automated customer engagement and CRM platform.

### 2. Co-Founding ADAI Circle
In 2020, Byamasu Patrick Paul and Amisi Hassan Jospin established ADAI Circle in Dzaleka to equip refugee youth with advanced skills in AI, data science, hardware maintenance, and software engineering. He helped build ADAI Circle's innovation hub, providing public internet access and hands-on hardware training.

---

## Impact

Through Rexplore Research Labs and ADAI Circle, Byamasu Patrick Paul has established himself as a pioneering AI founder in Malawi, proving that cutting-edge artificial intelligence and autonomous agent research can emerge directly from refugee community roots.
