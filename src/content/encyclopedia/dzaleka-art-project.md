## Overview

The Dzaleka Art Project is a documentation project by and about the artists who live as refugees and asylum seekers in Dzaleka. It gathers profiles of individual artists, images of their work, and writing about the place of the arts in their lives, and publishes them on a public website.

## History

Lisa Gilman, a professor of folklore and English at [George Mason University](https://iir.gmu.edu/articles/20051), had done research in Malawi since the 1990s but knew little about Dzaleka until she attended the [Tumaini Festival](/encyclopedia/tumaini-festival) in November 2021. The project grew out of that visit. Her account of the work, "A Community-Based Documentation Project in a Malawian Refugee Camp," was published in the [Journal of Folklore and Education](https://jfepublications.org/article/the-dzaleka-art-project/).

## Purpose

The project describes its aim as giving camp artists more chances to share their work and to be seen, and as countering stereotypes about refugees by showing the range of talent in Dzaleka. It documents the same arts scene associated with the Tumaini Festival and the refugee-led arts groups covered elsewhere in this encyclopedia.
