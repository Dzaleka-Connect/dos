## Early life

Njamba Koffi — published as John Michael Koffi — fled ethnic persecution in the Democratic Republic of the Congo with his family as a child and grew up in refugee camps: Dzaleka in Malawi, then Mpaka in Eswatini. His memoir [*Refuge-E: The Journey Much Desired*](/encyclopedia/refuge-e-book), published in April 2018, follows that route; the book has its own entry in this encyclopedia.

## Education

While at Mpaka, Koffi encountered the nearby Waterford Kamhlaba UWC school and applied to the [United World Colleges](https://www.uwc.org/news/?pid=22&nid=7&storyid=3533) movement. He was accepted on a full scholarship to UWC Robert Bosch College in Germany, which he attended from 2015 to 2017.

## Canada

Koffi went on to study International Relations and Creative Writing at the University of British Columbia, where UWC's alumni coverage describes him as a campus advocate for refugee awareness and African perspectives. His later work in Canada has included community-initiatives roles in the non-profit sector, board service, and speaking on refugee experiences.
