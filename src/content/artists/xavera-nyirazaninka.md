# Xavera Nyirazaninka

## About the Artist

Xavera Nyirazaninka has been living in the Dzaleka refugee camp in Malawi since November, 2004 when she came to seek asylum due to insecurity and war.

## Artistic Journey

She learned crafts in Dzaleka in 2010, and earns a living out of it in spite of a few challenges.

## Dzaleka Art Project

Xavera is one of the visual artists participating in the Dzaleka Art Project, which supports and promotes artistic expression within the refugee camp community.

## Artistic Community

As a member of this vibrant artistic community, Xavera contributes to the rich cultural landscape of Dzaleka through traditional crafts and artisanal work.
