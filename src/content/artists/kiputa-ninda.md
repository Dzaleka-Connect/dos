# Kiputa Ninda

## About the Artist

Kiputa Ninda and Riziki Nyembo fled from the Democratic Republic of the Congo because of war, and have been living in Dzaleka refugee camp since June 2015.

## Artistic Journey

Kiputa runs a carpentry shop in the camp, contributing to the community through his woodworking skills and business.

## Dzaleka Art Project

Kiputa is one of the visual artists participating in the Dzaleka Art Project, which supports and promotes artistic expression within the refugee camp community.

## Artistic Community

As a member of this vibrant artistic community, Kiputa contributes to the rich cultural landscape of Dzaleka through carpentry and woodworking, providing essential services to the community.
