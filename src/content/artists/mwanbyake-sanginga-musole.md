# Mwanbyake Sanginga Musole

## About the Artist

Mwanbyake Sanginga Musole is a young Congolese artist who fled his country of origin in 2014 due to war. He currently lives with his family in Dzaleka refugee camp.

## Artistic Journey

He makes and sells bracelets and other beadwork.

## Dzaleka Art Project

Mwanbyake is one of the visual artists participating in the Dzaleka Art Project, which supports and promotes artistic expression within the refugee camp community.

## Artistic Community

As a member of this vibrant artistic community, Mwanbyake contributes to the rich cultural landscape of Dzaleka through beadwork and jewelry making.
