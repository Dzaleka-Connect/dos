# Clementine Muniyo Shuti

## About the Artist

Muniyo Shuti Clementine has been living in Dzaleka since 2004, when she came with her husband and her children to seek asylum from war.

## Artistic Journey

She is a weaver and makes different kinds of baskets.

## Dzaleka Art Project

Clementine is one of the visual artists participating in the Dzaleka Art Project, which supports and promotes artistic expression within the refugee camp community.

## Artistic Community

As a member of this vibrant artistic community, Clementine contributes to the rich cultural landscape of Dzaleka through traditional weaving and basket making.

## Contact Information

- **Location**: Dzaleka Refugee Camp, Zone 1
- **Nationality**: Republic of Burundi
- **Age**: 45 years old
- **Specialties**: Weaver and basket maker
