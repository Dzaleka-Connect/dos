# Joseph Ibila Kililo

## About the Artist

Joseph Ibila Kililo is a young Artist born in 1997 in the Democratic Republic of Congo and is from South Kivu. He fled his country of origin in 2019 due to ethnic insecurity.

## Artistic Journey

He lost his father, which led to major threats to his life. He is a carpenter and sculptor.

## Dzaleka Art Project

Joseph is one of the visual artists participating in the Dzaleka Art Project, which supports and promotes artistic expression within the refugee camp community.

## Artistic Community

As a member of this vibrant artistic community, Joseph contributes to the rich cultural landscape of Dzaleka through carpentry and sculpture, creating 3D artworks.
