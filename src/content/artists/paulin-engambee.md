# Paulin Engambee

## About the Artist

Paulin Engambee has been living in Dzaleka, Malawi since March 2012, when he came to seek asylum due to insecurity. He is from Uvira in South-Kivu, Democratic Republic of Congo.

## Artistic Journey

He started drawing at the age of 10 and went to a painting school thereafter. He currently runs a home-based gallery where he draws and paints alongside his wife, who does various crafts.

## Challenges

Paulin is a talented artist who has participated in a number of exhibitions in and outside the camp. However he still faces a few challenges: low buying prices for his work, poor painting materials, etc.

## Dzaleka Art Project

Paulin is one of the visual artists participating in the Dzaleka Art Project, which supports and promotes artistic expression within the refugee camp community.

## Artistic Community

As a member of this vibrant artistic community, Paulin contributes to the rich cultural landscape of Dzaleka through visual arts and creative expression, despite the challenges of limited resources.
