# Richesse Kabamba

## About the Artist

RICHESSE KABAMBA is a young Congolese digital artist born in 1998. He started doing art in the Democratic Republic of the Congo in 2016, but he was forced to flee due to political insecurity.

## Personal Background

His father was killed for working on behalf of the opposition party, and the rest of the family was being hunted as well.

## Dzaleka Art Project

He contributed to digital art for the Dzaleka Arts Project banner, and he has helped with others aspects of the project.

## Artistic Community

As a member of this vibrant artistic community, Richesse contributes to the rich cultural landscape of Dzaleka through digital arts and creative expression.
