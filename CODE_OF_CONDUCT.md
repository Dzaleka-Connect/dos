# Dzaleka Online Services Code of Conduct

Dzaleka Online Services is a community-maintained open-source project. We want contributors, maintainers, users, community organisations, researchers and developers to be able to participate without harassment, intimidation or discrimination.

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone participating in the project.

## Our standards

We expect participants to:

* Treat other people with respect.
* Give constructive feedback and discuss ideas without attacking the person behind them.
* Respect differences in language, nationality, ethnicity, gender, disability, age, religion, experience and background.
* Respect people's privacy and personal information.
* Ask for permission before sharing photographs, personal stories or information about another person where consent is required.
* Be careful when contributing information about vulnerable people or communities.
* Correct inaccurate information when it is identified.
* Credit contributors and original sources where appropriate.
* Accept reasonable decisions made by project maintainers.
* Focus discussions on improving the project and serving its users.

Disagreement is welcome. Harassment is not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

* Harassment, threats, intimidation or personal attacks.
* Discriminatory or degrading comments.
* Hate speech or targeted abuse.
* Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Deliberately sharing false, misleading or manipulated information.
* Impersonating another person, organisation or project contributor.
* Repeatedly disrupting discussions, issues, pull requests or other project spaces.
* Spam, scams, malicious links or attempts to misuse project infrastructure.
* Using project spaces to exploit, threaten or endanger community members.
* Retaliating against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Community information and privacy

Dzaleka Online Services works with information that can relate to individuals, organisations and communities.

Contributors must take particular care when handling:

* names and contact details;
* photographs and videos;
* personal stories;
* information about children;
* information about protection or safeguarding concerns;
* information that could expose someone to harassment, discrimination or other harm.

Do not add sensitive personal information simply because it is available elsewhere.

If you are unsure whether information should be published, raise the question with the maintainers before submitting it.

## Project spaces

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in Dzaleka Online Services spaces, including:

* GitHub repositories;
* issues and pull requests;
* code reviews;
* documentation;
* project discussions;
* contribution forms;
* community channels managed by the project;
* project meetings and events;
* communications made while representing Dzaleka Online Services.

It may also apply to conduct outside these spaces when that conduct directly affects the safety of project participants or the integrity of the project.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaches this Code of Conduct, contact:

**Dzaleka Online Services**
Email: [dzalekaconnect@gmail.com](mailto:dzalekaconnect@gmail.com)
Website: https://services.dzaleka.com/

Include enough information for the issue to be understood, such as:

* what happened;
* where it happened;
* when it happened;
* links, screenshots or other relevant evidence, where available.

You do not need to confront the person involved before making a report.

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. Information will only be shared with people who need it to assess or respond to the concern.

Reports made deliberately to harass another participant or containing knowingly false information may themselves breach this Code of Conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, a response may include:

### 1. Guidance

For minor or unintentional behaviour, a maintainer may explain the issue and ask the participant to change their behaviour.

### 2. Warning

A formal warning may be issued where behaviour is inappropriate, repeated or more serious.

### 3. Temporary restriction

A participant may temporarily lose access to project discussions, contribution channels or other community spaces.

### 4. Permanent removal

Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent removal from project spaces and rejection of future contributions.

Maintainers may take immediate action where necessary to protect participants, community members, project infrastructure or sensitive information.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers should:

* apply this Code of Conduct fairly;
* consider available evidence before making decisions;
* avoid conflicts of interest where reasonably possible;
* protect the privacy of people making reports;
* document significant enforcement decisions where appropriate;
* prioritise the safety of affected people over arguments about intent.

A maintainer who is directly involved in a complaint should not be the sole person deciding its outcome where another maintainer is available.

## Good-faith contributions

People make mistakes.

Where appropriate, contributors should be given an opportunity to understand a problem, correct it and improve their future participation.

This does not require the project to tolerate harassment, threats, deliberate abuse or conduct that creates a serious safety risk.
